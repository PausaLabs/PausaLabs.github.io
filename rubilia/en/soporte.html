Rubilia Support

Contact

Write to pausalabs@gmail.com for questions, problems, purchases or privacy requests. Please include your language, your device and, if relevant, the level number.

Purchases

  • The "Full version" is a one-time purchase that adds 80 levels and removes ads. Recover it on another device or after reinstalling with "Restore purchases" and the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds the purchase, the paid content and ad removal are deactivated; your progress is kept.

Leaderboard data

  • In the leaderboard or in "About", "Delete my leaderboard data" deletes your alias, results, attempts, purchase verification and anonymous account from the server. Your progress, settings and purchase don't change.
  • You can also request it by writing to pausalabs@gmail.com.

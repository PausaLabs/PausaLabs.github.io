Rubilia Privacy Notice

Version 1.1 · Effective November 9, 2026

Translation pending review. If this translation and the Spanish version differ, the Spanish version prevails to the extent permitted by law.

1. Responsible party

Jesús Adrián Suárez Rojas, an individual who uses Pausa Labs as a trade name, with an address for notices at pausalabs@gmail.com, is responsible for processing the personal data obtained through Rubilia. Privacy contact: pausalabs@gmail.com.

This notice is issued under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares) and describes how the app actually works.

2. Summary

  • You do not need to create an account or give us your name, email or phone number to play.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Online leaderboards are optional and use an anonymous identifier and the alias you choose.
  • Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob. The app does not request tracking permission (ATT) and does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not process sensitive personal data, we do not sell your data and we do not use it to build our own profiles.

3. Data stored only on your device

Progress, records, scores and local statistics; the game in progress; theme, language, magnifier and sound; the game counter used for ad frequency; a cache of your purchase status verified by StoreKit; and the version, date (UTC) and language in which you accepted the Terms of Use. This data is not sent to our servers. If you use the leaderboards, your alias, your choice to participate and the anonymous session credentials are also stored on the device.

4. Data we process and why

Online leaderboards (only if you choose "Join"):

  • Data: a random anonymous session identifier (Supabase Auth) and its credentials; public alias; participation choice and the dates you joined and changed your alias; for each attempt, the level, mode, rules, type of help and controls, level signature, start time recorded by the server and result (time or score).
  • Purposes: showing the leaderboards, calculating positions, validating that results are legitimate and preventing cheating and abuse.

Purchase verification for paid leaderboards (only if you participate and own the "Full version"):

  • Data: the Apple-signed transaction (StoreKit 2), with its original transaction identifier, product, environment and verification or revocation dates, linked to your anonymous identifier.
  • Purpose: checking with Apple's signature that the purchase is genuine before accepting results on paid levels, and preventing the same purchase from being used on several accounts.

Service security:

  • Data: the IP address of the connection.
  • Purpose: limiting the number of requests and protecting the server. Our code uses it only in memory and does not store it in the database; Supabase technical logs may include it for their limited retention period.

All of these purposes are necessary to provide the service you request. We do not process your data for secondary purposes such as our own marketing.

5. Providers and third parties

  • Supabase (database and authentication for the leaderboards; infrastructure in the Amazon Web Services us-east-1 region, in the United States) processes leaderboard data on our behalf, as a processor, under its data processing agreement.
  • Apple processes downloads, payments, refunds and purchase restoration as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy. We receive only the signed transaction needed to verify the purchase, never your payment method details.
  • Google (AdMob and its consent messaging platform, UMP) receives data directly from the ads SDK when ads are shown in the free version: IP address and approximate location derived from it, device identifiers limited to the app or developer, information about the ad and your interaction with it, and technical and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to serve, measure and protect ads under its policy (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the law requires it, Google first asks for your consent to personalized ads through UMP; accepting the Terms of Use is not that consent. With the "Full version", no ads are requested or shown.

Apart from these cases, we will only disclose data to authorities when a law or a competent order requires it.

6. International transfers

Leaderboard data is hosted in the United States. Apple and Google may process data in different countries under their policies. Using the leaderboards or the ad-supported version involves these disclosures, which are necessary to provide those services.

7. Retention

  • Device data: until you delete it, reset the app or uninstall it.
  • Leaderboard and purchase verification data: while your leaderboard session exists and as long as it is needed for the leaderboards and fraud prevention. If you stop participating, your alias and results are no longer shown on the leaderboards; you can ask for them to be deleted by writing to pausalabs@gmail.com.
  • Immediate deletion: "Delete my leaderboard data" (in the leaderboard or in "About") instantly deletes your alias, results, attempts, purchase verification and anonymous account from the server, and earlier sessions stop working. No copy is kept in the database; provider technical logs follow their own limited period.
  • Provider technical logs: for the limited periods set by their policies.
  • Data a law requires us to keep: for the period that law establishes.

When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized.

8. Your choices

  • Leaderboards: participating is optional. You can stop with "Stop taking part".
  • Advertising: when Google requires it for your region, "About" shows "Ad privacy" so you can review or withdraw your consent. Buying the "Full version" removes ads.
  • Tracking: the app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps.
  • You can erase all data on the device by uninstalling the app.
  • Deletion: "Delete my leaderboard data" deletes your data from the server and closes your anonymous session; your progress, settings and purchase don't change, and you won't take part again unless you choose to.

9. ARCO rights and withdrawal of consent

You can access, rectify or cancel your data or object to its processing (ARCO rights), and withdraw your consent or limit the use of your data, by writing to pausalabs@gmail.com. Please include:

  • your name or that of your representative and a way to reply to you;
  • the right you are exercising and a description of the data;
  • your leaderboard alias and, if possible, approximate dates of use, so we can locate the anonymous identifier. Because we do not ask for identity data, we may ask you to confirm from the app that the session is yours.

We will reply within 20 days and, where appropriate, carry out the request within the following 15 days; these periods may be extended once in the cases provided by law. The procedure is free of charge. If you believe your request was not handled properly, you may contact the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, the Mexican authority for personal data held by private parties.

If you live in another jurisdiction, you may have additional rights under your local law; we will handle your requests through the same channel.

10. Minors

Rubilia does not ask for identity data and is not intended to collect data from minors. If you are a minor, use the app with the permission of your parent or guardian, and do not use your real name or personal data as your alias. If you believe a minor has given us personal data, write to us so we can delete it.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections, server-side purchase verification with Apple's signature, limited session credentials and access controls on the database. No measure is infallible, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; if a breach significantly affecting your rights occurs, we will inform you as required by law.

12. Changes to this notice

We will inform you of any change in the app, in "About" and at https://pausalabs.github.io/rubilia/. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it before applying it.
